Guinea pig cage sizes

Two sows10.5 sq ft (1 m²), e.g. 76 × 130 cm (30 × 51 in)
Two boars12 sq ft (1.1 m²), e.g. 183 × 61 cm (6 × 2 ft)
Three guinea pigs13 sq ft (1.2 m²), e.g. 76 × 159 cm (30 × 63 in)
Four guinea pigs16 sq ft (1.5 m²), e.g. 76 × 196 cm (30 × 77 in)
HeightSides 30 cm (12 in) or more
LevelsFloor space counts; lofts do not
FloorSolid, never wire

Rat cage sizes

Minimum for up to 4 rats80 × 50 × 80 cm (31.5 × 20 × 31.5 in) (Woodgreen)
Recommended for 2 to 5 rats90 × 60 × 120 cm (35 × 24 × 47 in) living space (Blue Cross)
Bar spacing1 cm (0.4 in); no rat should fit its head through (Woodgreen, RSPCA)
FloorSolid, not wire mesh (RSPCA, PDSA)
Room temperature19 to 23°C (66 to 73°F) (RSPCA)
HidesOne more than the number of rats, each with two exits (RSPCA)
Number of ratsAt least two; never one alone
Time out of the cageAn hour or so a day, supervised (RSPCA)
